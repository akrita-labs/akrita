The Triumvirate

Three signed agents, one frontier

AKRITA runs as three autonomous keepers. Each proposes actions independently, each is gated by a deterministic Risk Agent, and each writes a verifiable reasoning trace before anything executes.

NOMOS — claim issuer

Inscribes the claim

Reads real on-chain rug signals — stablecoin freezes, token-security failures — and issues a signed rug-risk claim, writing the reasoning trace before it is anchored on Arc.

SPATHA — risk sentinel

Contains exposure

Stakes the two-sided bond market for or against each claim when the Risk Agent allows it, so the losing side is slashed on resolution.

AGROS — treasury agent

Keeps idle capital working

Parks idle bond collateral in USYC for treasury yield between resolutions — always preserving the audit trail.
