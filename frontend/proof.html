The Proof Layer

Every decision leaves a verifiable trace

No agent acts on trust. Each verdict is committed as a canonical reasoning trace and anchored on Arc, so anyone can replay the chain from decision to on-chain proof.

  1. DecisionAn agent proposes an action under the Risk Agent's deterministic gates.
  2. HashThe reasoning is serialized to canonical JSON and fingerprinted with sha256.
  3. IPFSThe full trace is pinned via Circle Nanopayments and addressed by CID.
  4. Arc AnchorThe hash and CID are committed to the TraceRegistry on Arc.
  5. VerificationAnyone re-hashes the trace and checks it against the on-chain anchor.
