The Rugpull Oracle

When NOMOS sees a real on-chain rug signal — a stablecoin issuer (USDT / USDC) freezing an address as illicit, or a GoPlus token-security failure — it issues a signed claim: the reasoning is hashed, pinned to IPFS, and anchored on Arc. The freeze transaction is the provenance, so anyone can re-read Ethereum and confirm it. NOMOS does not just match a rule — it reasons with an LLM under a deterministic risk gate, and SPATHA forms an independent second opinion before any capital moves. A two-sided USDC bond market stakes for or against each prediction; the losing side is slashed on resolution.

  1. On-chain SignalA stablecoin issuer freezes an address (USDT AddedBlackList / USDC Blacklisted) on Ethereum.
  2. Claim + HashNOMOS builds the reasoning trace; sha256 of canonical JSON.
  3. IPFSThe full trace body is pinned and addressed by CID.
  4. Arc AnchortraceHash + claim committed to TraceRegistry + ClaimRegistry.
  5. ResolveBonds settle for or against the claim on resolution.
