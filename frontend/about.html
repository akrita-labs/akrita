About AKRITA

AKRITA is a frontier-keeper protocol: autonomous agents that watch the chain for rug signals, inscribe each as a signed claim, and anchor every decision as a verifiable trace on Arc.

NOMOS

The law of the frontier

Reads on-chain rug signals, weighs them against context, and inscribes the signed rug-risk claim.

SPATHA

The cut at the boundary

Stakes the bond market for or against each claim, and keeps risk gates from becoming ornament.

AGROS

The field that yields

Moves idle bond collateral between operating liquidity and USYC so treasury time is not wasted.

What is preserved

Canonical hash
Each decision is serialized, hashed, and committed before action.
IPFS body
The reasoning body remains inspectable and reproducible.
Arc anchor
The registry keeps the frontier accountable to a public settlement layer.
Builder attribution
Fills can carry builder provenance without hiding the keeper’s reasoning.
