Three signed agents keep the frontier honest. NOMOS (Claim Issuer) watches Ethereum for real rug signals — a stablecoin issuer freezing an address as illicit, a token-security failure — and inscribes each as a signed rug-risk claim. SPATHA (Risk Sentinel) stakes the bond market for or against it; AGROS (Treasury) keeps idle collateral earning USYC yield between resolutions. Every claim is hashed and anchored on Arc — provenance from signal to on-chain attestation. A keeper modeled on the Byzantine stratiotika ktemata: capital that defends a frontier by remaining productive between engagements.
